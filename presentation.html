Problem Context

Scale growth does not remove station-level risk

Fast charging-infrastructure growth does not guarantee stable cash flow.
Demand, service duration, electricity price, service fee and dwell time vary simultaneously.
Risk metrics reveal loss-making days hidden by averages.

Conclusions & Recommendations

Operating actions from risk results

BA3093 Simulation and Risk Analysis
Recommendation 01

Congestion-based dynamic service fees

When peak-hour utilization exceeds ρ = 0.5, introduce a 15%-25% peak premium.

Recommendation 02

Preventive expansion trigger

If rolling 30-day peak ρ > 0.5 persists, start procurement and site planning.

Recommendation 03

Wholesale cost hedge

Use fixed or capped-price contracts for 50%-60% of expected monthly consumption.
